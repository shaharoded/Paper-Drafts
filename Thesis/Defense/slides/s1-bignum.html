About 1 minute. Lead with the number, then the table on the next slide. INTERVenE-Enc reaches a support-weighted AUPRC of 0.672 across the six outcomes. That is +0.041 over the strongest neural baseline (ss-STraTS, 0.631) and +0.085 over KarmaLego+TPF (0.587), both with non-overlapping 95% bootstrap confidence intervals. It also has the best AUROC, F1 and length-of-stay error. Why both deltas matter: the KarmaLego gap is the one that isolates modeling from representation, because both consume the identical KBTA interval stream. The ss-STraTS gap is the raw versus abstracted comparison inside the neural family, holding the sequence-model budget in the same family. AUPRC is the primary metric because outcomes are imbalanced (prevalence from 2.7% to 26.4%).
