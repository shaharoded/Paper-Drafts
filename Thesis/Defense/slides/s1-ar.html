About 1 minute. Two representative test patients from INTERVenE-Ar (Appendix B, autoregressive risk trajectories). The decoder is seeded with the first 48 hours and generates the abstraction trajectory forward, reading death risk at every generated step. For the patient who died, generated risk rises progressively and peaks near the recorded death time (around hour 170). For the survivor it stays near baseline throughout. The saw-tooth in the red curve is token-level: each generated token updates the readout. Why keep the decoder despite lower headline numbers: it is the only variant that localizes when, and after which events, risk rises, and its explanation is the generated trajectory itself, with no attribution machinery. The encoder is the production predictor (3.3 ms), the decoder the inspection tool (255 ms). If asked about calibration: the decoder's probability is the maximum over a generated trajectory, a readout with no reason to be calibrated as emitted (ECE 4 to 12%); per-outcome temperature scaling fixes the prevalent outcomes.
