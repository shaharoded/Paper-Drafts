About 1.5 minutes. INTERVenE-Enc: full bidirectional attention, trading the ability to generate for a single-pass discriminative readout. One pass gives, per admission and outcome, a risk probability (sigmoid) and a time estimate (softplus). RELEASE is reported separately as length of stay. 3.3 ms per patient at batch 16 on one RTX A5000, 302 patients per second, the whole 8,562-patient test set in under 30 seconds. INTERVenE-Ar: causal mask. Seeded with the observed window, it generates freely forward in time and reads the risk head at every generated step, giving a per-complication probability curve. Generation stops via an inference gate driven by the model's own time-to-terminal estimate; patients reaching the cap get a forced terminal token (DEATH or RELEASE by highest logit); a 2,000-token safety budget. 255 ms per patient, 3.9 patients per second, impractical on CPU. The roughly 77x gap is simply generation: one pass versus one pass per token. They are complementary rather than redundant: the encoder is the production predictor, the decoder the inspection tool. Only the decoder localizes when, and after which events, risk rises. Each has its own explanation route: sparse-transcoder attribution for the encoder, direct reading of generated tokens for the decoder.
