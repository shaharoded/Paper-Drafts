"Best baseline" is the best of GRU-D, STraTS (pre+ft), ss-STraTS and KarmaLego for that outcome: GRU-D for kidney, ss-STraTS for hyperglycemia, severe hyperglycemia and death, STraTS (pre+ft) for hypoglycemia and severe hypoglycemia. Largest gains: kidney complication (0.790 vs 0.729 for GRU-D; vs 0.726 for ss-STraTS) and death (0.605 vs 0.563). On the rarest outcome, severe hypoglycemia at 2.7%, it ties STraTS with overlapping intervals: there, scarcity of positives sets the ceiling rather than modeling. Prevalence is the AUPRC random baseline, so 0.144 on a 2.7% outcome is still about five times chance. The time head is a separate capability from ranking: a model can identify who will deteriorate without saying when. Against the median-time baseline, onset MAE drops by roughly 7 to 14 hours across outcomes (e.g. kidney 28.8 to 18.4 h, death 136.9 to 123.0 h). INTERVenE-Ar's biggest weakness is death: AUPRC 0.368 against the encoder's 0.605, the largest gap between the two variants. Dysglycemia outcomes, which occur earlier and more often, are where the generative readout holds up. Calibration if asked (Appendix): encoder ECE 0.016 as emitted, 0.013 after temperature scaling; decoder 4 to 12% under its peak readout, scaling fixes the prevalent outcomes (death 0.050 to 0.011).
