Eligibility: inpatient visit with evidence of an overnight stay (a measurement between 18:00 and 07:00), length of stay 48 h to 14 days, age ≥ 18, not in excluded departments (pediatrics, obstetrics, which also removes gestational diabetes), no visit ID shared across patients, no death within 48 h, and at least one of three criteria: documented diabetes diagnosis, recurrent hyperglycemia (two readings ≥ 180 mg/dL 2 to 24 h apart), or extreme hyperglycemia (any reading ≥ 250 mg/dL). 98,050 admissions met the clinical criteria; restricting to August 2022 to December 2025 gives 48,354. Cohort: mean age 71.9, 54.3% male, median stay 5.55 days, 30-day mortality 9.58%. Center sizes 4,478 to 11,024 admissions. Pre-period 21,605 admissions; post-period 16,182. The design point: only 35.3% had a documented diabetes diagnosis, and only 21.5% of admissions meeting a hyperglycemia criterion carried one. A cohort defined by the recorded diagnosis, as is conventional, would have excluded roughly four in five of them, not because they lacked significant inpatient hyperglycemia, but because no one entered a code. Measurement-based criteria recover them. Also: adherence was essentially the same whether or not diabetes was documented (0.669 vs 0.661). Undocumented patients are not managed differently, they are just harder to find.
