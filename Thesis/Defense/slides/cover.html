About 1 minute. Thank the committee for coming. Introduce yourself and Yuval as advisor. One-sentence framing: this thesis asks whether short-horizon complication prediction for hospitalized patients can be framed as temporal next-token prediction over a stream of clinically named intervals, and, on the same representation, whether adherence to the national inpatient diabetes guideline can be measured automatically at national scale. Say up front that the talk has three studies: a methodological study on MIMIC-IV, a clinical study across six Israeli hospitals (Kineret), and a final experiment joining the two. Then contributions and where the work was published.
