About 1.5 minutes. Support-weighted across the six outcomes, 95% bootstrap CIs are about ±0.012 to 0.014 on AUPRC. F1 (not shown): Enc 0.628, best baseline 0.618. Points to make: 1. KarmaLego+TPF is the weakest single-pass model and its length-of-stay error is more than twice any neural baseline. It reads the same intervals as INTERVenE-Enc, so that gap is the cleanest modeling comparison in the table. 2. Pretraining did not help STraTS here: supervised-only (0.631) beat pretrain-then-fine-tune (0.614), which is why ss-STraTS is the reference neural baseline throughout. 3. INTERVenE-Ar's numbers are close to KarmaLego on discrimination but are read under a strictly harder contract: predictions only after a full autoregressive rollout, length of stay read from the terminal generated state. Reported for completeness, not as a like-for-like comparison. Its strongest outcomes are the dysglycemia ones; death is hard for it. Parameter counts if asked: GRU-D 47.9k, STraTS ~87–93k, INTERVenE ~1.8M; KarmaLego uses 25.6k selected sparse pattern features.
