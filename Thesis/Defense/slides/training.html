About 2 minutes. Pick the details the committee is likely to probe. Phase 1, both variants: train the embedder alone with a temporal next-token objective, a multi-hot BCE over the set of events in a short future window plus an MSE on time to the next event. A set rather than a single next token, because events minutes apart have no meaningful order. The Δt term is held behind a BCE-only warmup, then its weight is calibrated once from the loss ratio and capped. That calibrate-once-and-cap discipline applies to every auxiliary loss. Phase 2, encoder: masked LM with atomic-interval masking, about 15% of positions, START and END of an interval masked together with dedicated tokens (masking them independently would make the task trivial). Two time-aware auxiliaries: t_pos regresses time since admission, t_local regresses the gap to neighbours at masked positions. Phase 2, decoder: teacher-forced next-token prediction with two changes. Legality masking: a boolean mask from sequence state forbids continuations the TAK grammar disallows (END before START, conflicting open states, meal order), applied to logits and targets and again at generation. Soft-kernel temporal targets: terminal events are rare and late, so the one-hot target is replaced by exp(−Δt/τ) within a horizon (τ terminal 12 h, outcomes initialised at 48 h and learnable). Plus time-to-terminal regression, a pairwise ranking loss, and curriculum-by-masking so it tolerates its own imperfect context. Phase 3, encoder: per-outcome attention pool, shared MLP, BCE on risk with prevalence-based positive weights plus λ·MSE on hours to first occurrence (λ = 0.1, the most consequential hyperparameter). Backbone at a small fraction of the head learning rate. Phase 3, decoder: fine-tune the risk head on natural-distribution batches to close the teacher-forcing versus free-running mismatch, backbone at 0.01 of the head learning rate. Search: no automated optimizer. Iterative design search on a 10,000-admission subset, then confirmation on the full cohort; every arm that informed a decision is in Appendix A (ablations).
