- Phase 1 (shared by both variants): train the embedder alone. Target = the set of events in a short future window (multi-hot BCE), because events minutes apart have no real order. Plus MSE on the time to the next event, switched on after a BCE-only warm-up; its weight is calibrated once from the loss ratio and capped (same rule for every auxiliary loss) - Masked LM (phase 2): ~15% of positions hidden; an interval's START and END are hidden together with a [MASK_INTERVAL] token, then full-vocabulary cross-entropy at the hidden positions. Hiding one end alone is trivial: the other end gives it away - t_pos: at every position, regress time since admission. t_local: at hidden positions, regress the gap to the nearest neighbour (in days). Keeps time inside the hidden states, so a hidden token still knows where it sits. Both join after a few MLM-only epochs, capped at a fraction of the MLM loss - Phase 3 loss: plain multi-label BCE on risk (no class weights) + 0.1 × per-outcome MSE on hours to onset, positives only, z-normalized so long-LoS outcomes don't dominate. The RELEASE time slot doubles as the length-of-stay predictor. λ = 0.1 keeps risk dominant; larger λ cost discrimination - Curriculum-by-masking: randomly hide 15% of input tokens during phase 3, interval ends together. A regularizer so the heads don't lean on any single token - Backbone at 0.01 × the head learning rate: the heads adapt, the pretrained representation stays. Freezing it loses 0.068 AUPRC; 0.1 × also hurts - Dropped arms are in the caption and the encoder supplementary slide, not part of the method
