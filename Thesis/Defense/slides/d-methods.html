Methods

Knowledge base, Mediator, INTERVenE

Transition to methods, the core of the talk (about 13 minutes for this section). Frame it: the work is two studies that share a front end. Both convert an OMOP-formatted record into a stream of KBTA intervals using the same knowledge base and the same abstraction engine, and diverge afterwards: one into predictive modeling, the other into adherence scoring over the same intervals.
