Ben-Gurion University of the Negev · Shahar Oded

28

About 1.5 minutes. Overall adherence rose from 0.682 before the guideline to 0.703 after (P < .001), with monthly scores trending upward. The absolute change is small against 37,787 admissions in the two periods, which is why the monthly series is shown next to the P value. The dotted lines mark the transition year excluded from the primary comparison (the rollout of the position paper, the mandatory e-learning module and the new quality indicator). Component level, uneven: - Glucose monitoring improved most: on admission 0.78 to 0.93; routine 0.83 to 0.94. - Discontinuing high-hypoglycemia-risk antidiabetics on admission rose from 0.62 to 0.75. - Insulin-management components: statistically significant but marginal, +0.01 to +0.03, still close to their low baselines (insulin after hyperglycemia 0.14 to 0.15). - Dose reduction after hypoglycemia did not change; SGLT2 discontinuation was already complete. The pattern is coherent: the implementation moved the recommendations that were already easy to follow and left the hard ones roughly where they were. Caveat: an uncontrolled pre–post comparison, so secular trends cannot be separated from the implementation itself.
