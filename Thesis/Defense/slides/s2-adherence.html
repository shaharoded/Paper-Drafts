About 2 minutes. This is the study's central descriptive finding. Mean adherence per recommendation over the whole study period, 0 = non-adherence, 1 = complete adherence. Adherence follows the kind of action required, not its clinical weight. Single discrete actions on admission are near-universal: SGLT2 discontinuation is complete (1.00) and creatinine almost so (0.98). Recommendations that call for repeated insulin decisions across the stay are not: insulin after hyperglycemia 0.15, the two routine basal components 0.21 and 0.23, bolus reduction after hypoglycemia 0.28. All four have a median of zero: in most eligible admissions the expected action was absent altogether. Monitoring and admission medication management fall in between, 0.54 to 0.88. Why the fuzzy grading matters here: a binary verdict would lump "followed late" with "never followed". The trapezoids separate them, and the medians of zero show that the low scores are genuine absence, not accumulated lateness. Reminder of what a low score means: the recommendation applied and was not followed. A recommendation that never became applicable produces no score.
