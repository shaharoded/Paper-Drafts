About 1.5 minutes. The knowledge base was authored for this thesis. It encodes the clinical semantics of adult inpatient diabetes care (raw concepts, states, trends, events, contexts, patterns) in the TAK schema, and encodes the Israeli national position paper on diabetes treatment in general-hospital admissions (Hochberg et al., September 2023) as quality-assessment patterns on top. Written by me under Yuval's guidance, with Dr. Irit Hochberg reviewing the formalized recommendations for clinical fidelity against the position paper. Knowledge acquisition in two passes: a data-driven pass (1-D k-means with 2 to 5 clusters, KDE plots to find dips between peaks) that only proposes candidate thresholds, then an authoritative clinical pass against reference ranges, the guideline's own definitions, literature and expert input. Relevance durations are context-sensitive. Corticosteroids were pre-discretized because there are too many preparation and dose combinations. Two design goals: transferable (it names clinical concepts, not dataset columns, so a new EHR needs only a concept index) and single-schema (declarative and QA knowledge in one XML representation, validated against a shared schema, executed by one engine in one pass, rather than split between a declarative formalism and a procedural one). Caveat if asked: 18 knowledge-base concepts have no MIMIC-IV counterpart (home medications, SGLT2, cultures, imaging, dialysis, eGFR), which is part of why QA is confined to Kineret.
