\section{Target Support}
\label{app:kineret-support}

Ten complications were carried as candidates. Eight clear the $1\%$ training-prevalence threshold at $K = 4$ and keep a prediction head; hyperosmolality ($0.50\%$) and ketoacidosis ($0.75\%$) do not and are dropped. Support falls as $K$ rises, because the label window shortens and admissions whose complication precedes the cutoff are excluded.
