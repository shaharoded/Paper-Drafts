Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript, "Automated, Component-Level Assessment of a National Inpatient Diabetes Guideline: Adherence and Its Association with Hospitalization Complications Across Six Hospitals," as a Research and Applications article in the Journal of the American Medical Informatics Association.

The study applies automated temporal assessment to 14 guideline recommendations across 48,354 admissions in six hospitals, examines changes following national guideline publication, and evaluates associations between adherence and subsequent complications. The work contributes a reusable knowledge base and an approach to assessing implementation at hospital-network scale.

A version of this work is available as a medRxiv preprint: Oded S, Shahar Y, Shalom E, et al. medRxiv 2026. doi:10.64898/2026.09.26.26364069 (https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.26.26364069).

Generative AI tools were used as a writing assistant and to support methodological research and code adaptation. Every design choice, analysis step, and interpretation was directed and verified by the corresponding author and, in turn, by the co-authors, as disclosed in the manuscript.

Several authors contributed to the national position paper evaluated in this study. Funding, ethics approvals, and the authors' competing-interest statement are detailed in the manuscript. The uploaded supplementary PDF includes the operational definitions.
